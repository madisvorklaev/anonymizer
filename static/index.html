PDF Anonymizer

Runs locally. Nothing leaves this machine.

Upload a PDF, then drag rectangles on the page over anything to redact.
0 regions selected

Upload a previously anonymized PDF. The app reads the redaction IDs stamped into it, looks up the encrypted originals in its local database, and pastes them back to produce a restored file.

Redaction snapshots are encrypted and stored locally in data/redactions.db. Restoring only works from this same machine/installation.
